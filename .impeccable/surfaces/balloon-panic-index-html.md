# Balloon Panic (balloon-panic/index.html)

Scope: a new game page inside My Paint Studio's established world. Mode: Experience (the game is the work; menus recede).

Audience and job: children 2–6 with parents or siblings on one tablet or phone. They pass a growing balloon (or silly bomb) before it pops. Pictures, colour, sound and voice carry every instruction.

Constraints: plain HTML/CSS/JS + Phaser 3, offline, Arabic (RTL) and English, fixed player colours, touch targets 64px+, nothing frightening, nobody eliminated.

Decisions taken with the user: the family stickers are replaced in this game by an original cast of six code-drawn, fully animated buddies; a match is a fixed number of rounds followed by silly awards; a party soundtrack was requested.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The balloon is the star. One glossy, swelling object under a spotlight, four buddies around it reacting with their whole bodies. Refuses the "HUD-heavy arcade" arrangement: no timers, bars or numbers predicting the pop.

OWN-WORLD: The app's incumbent family (Fredoka / Baloo Bhaijaan 2, frosted glass controls, chunky ink-edged sunny "go" button), tinted for a party stage: drenched violet room (#5a2bd4 to #22105f), pink spotlight, turning light beams, violet ink #24124d, the four fixed player colours on podiums, glows and neckerchiefs.

STORY: See the demo behind the title, tap Play, tap your buddy, pass it, laugh at whoever it pops on, play again.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-screen live party. Title in sunny ink-stroked type centre, Play below; the buddies pass a balloon behind a soft veil. In a match: players on colour podiums at the edges, the balloon held toward the middle, pause and round dots top centre.

FORM: Extension of the Monkey Mischief page structure (always-on Phaser stage behind DOM menus); structure pinned by the user's brief, no concept round run. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
